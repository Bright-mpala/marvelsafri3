What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser to remember settings and improve site functionality.

Why We Use Cookies

Cookie Categories

  • Essential: Required for authentication, booking flow, and core platform behavior.
  • Performance: Helps identify errors and optimize speed, stability, and usability.
  • Preference: Saves selected language, device settings, and browsing preferences.
  • Marketing: Supports campaign measurement and relevant promotional content.
